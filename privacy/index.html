CLOCK-Wise — Privacy Policy

Demo model — Not for business use

Last updated: 22 November 2026

CLOCK-Wise ("the app") is a Windows app that records staff clock-in and clock-out times using a fingerprint reader. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

Summary

  • All data stays on the computer where the app is installed.
  • The app does not send any data to us or to anyone else. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • The app is a demonstration model and is not intended for business, payroll or legal record-keeping.

Data the app stores

  • Staff details entered by the administrator: employee code and name.
  • Fingerprint templates: when a finger is enrolled, the app converts the fingerprint image into a mathematical template (minutiae points) and stores only that template. Fingerprint images are not saved.
  • Attendance records: date and time of each clock-in and clock-out, and whether it was a scan or a manual entry.
  • Admin PIN: stored only as a one-way salted hash, never as plain text.

This data is kept in a local database file in the Windows user profile on that computer.

How the data is used

Only to identify enrolled staff at the reader, record their attendance, and produce attendance reports that the administrator chooses to save.

Sharing

The app does not upload, sell or share any data. Reports are only created when the administrator saves a CSV file, and that file is kept wherever the administrator saves it.

Fingerprint reader software

The app reads fingerprints through the separately installed HID Authentication Device Client from HID Global, which runs on the same computer. That software is covered by HID Global's own terms and privacy policy.

Your responsibilities as the administrator

Fingerprints are biometric data. If you use the app with real people, you are responsible for telling them, getting any consent your local law requires, and keeping the computer secure.

Deleting data

  • Use Staff → Clear fingers to delete an employee's fingerprint templates.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its local database and settings from that Windows user profile.

Children

The app is not directed at children.
